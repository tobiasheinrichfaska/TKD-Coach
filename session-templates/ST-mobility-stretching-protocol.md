# 🤸 Mobility, Warm-Up & Stretching Protocol
**TKD-focused. Personal use + adaptable for coaching (adults + kids).**
_Built 31.05.2026._

---

## How the phases fit together

| | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 + Phase 2 | **THE WARM-UP** — Mobility Floor + Dynamic (standing), combined | Before training |
| Phase 3 | **TRAIN** | — |
| Phase 4 | **STATIC BLOCK** — long holds | After training, or standalone when warm |
| Phase 5 | **MEDITATION** — slow-exhale breathing, parasympathetic downshift | Close of session |

**Day-to-day at home (no training):** Lite warm-up → Phase 4 static block.

---

## Breathing — three tools, three jobs

| Breath | Pattern | Job | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Triangle** | inhale → hold → exhale (equal counts) | Center & focus — arrive, transition into training | Start of Phase 1 (~4–6 rounds, short) |
| **Extended-exhale** (+ Klangschale pacer) | exhale longer than inhale (in 4 → out 6–8), ~5–6 breaths/min | Parasympathetic recovery — downshift, support HRV/sleep | Phase 5 |
| **Box** | inhale → hold → exhale → hold (equal counts) | Arousal regulation under pressure | As-needed — pre-comp/pre-spar nerves, off-mat stress (not a phase) |

---

## Core Principles

1. Warm-up (Phase 1+2) before, static (Phase 4) after. **Static before explosive work blunts power — never stretch statically before kicking.**
2. **Never stretch cold.** Warm up first, or do the static block post-session.
3. **CARs/circles — quality over count:** reach true end range + brace the whole body (irradiation). 2–3 deliberate reps beat 10 sloppy.
4. **Neuroathletik triad:** vision (neck + refocus), vestibular (neck + balance), proprioception (single-leg, eyes closed).

## PHASE 1 — Mobility Floor (part of warm-up)

**Open with ~4–6 rounds of triangle breathing** (arrive, focus; short — centering not sedating).

| # | Movement | Dose | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ankle circles | 2–3 ea. direction, both feet | Ankle (talocrural/subtalar); tib. ant., peroneals, gastroc/soleus via Achilles, ligaments |
| 2 | Knee circles | 2–3 ea. — gentle, small amplitude | Knee (tibiofemoral/patellofemoral); patellar tendon. Hinge — no cranking |
| 3 | Hip CARs (quadruped) | 2–3 ea. — slow, spine quiet | Hip + capsule; glutes, iliopsoas, adductors, deep ext. rotators |
| 4 | Scorpion (quadruped, dynamic) | 5–6/side | Hip + lumbar/thoracic spine (multi-plane): hip flexors, glutes, erectors, obliques, anterior chain |
| 5 | Spinal flow: cat → puppy → cow → cobra → cat, then reverse | 1–2 passes, continuous | Whole spine; puppy = lats/posterior shoulder/thoracic ext.; cobra = rectus abdominis, hip flexor, pec/ant. shoulder |

**Order:** ankle → knee circles → hip CARs → scorpion → spinal flow.

## PHASE 2 — Dynamic, standing (part of warm-up)

| # | Movement | Dose | Covers / stream |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leg swings front–back | 10/leg | Hip; hamstrings/hip flexors/quads/glutes |
| 2 | Leg swings lateral | 10/leg | Hip abd/add; adductors, glute med/TFL. Direct roundhouse/side-kick prep |
| 3 | Knee-to-chest + heel-to-glute | ~10m walking (or march in place) | Hip flexion (glutes/hamstring/hip flexor); quad/hip flexor + knee flexion |
| 4 | Footwork/skip/shuffle | 30–60s | Whole-body, calves/ankles — raises HR |
| 5 | Neck rotations + refocusing | gentle | Cervical spine; SCM/scalenes/traps/deep neck flexors. Vision + vestibular |
| 6 | Single-leg balance, eyes open→closed | 20–30s/side | Ankle/foot proprioceptors, glute-med, core. Proprioception + vestibular |

## PHASE 4 — Static Block (post-session, or standalone when warm)
_30–60s per hold._

| # | Stretch | How | Covers / tracked measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standing forward fold | hinge & hang | Posterior chain: hamstrings, gastroc, erectors → **Forward fold** |
| 2 | Seated forward fold | reach past toes | Hamstrings, calves, low back → **Sit-and-reach** |
| 3 | Half-kneeling hip flexor lunge (+reach back) | 30–60s/side | Iliopsoas + rectus femoris; anterior trunk → **Front split** |
| 4 | Seated straddle/pancake (PNF) | back flat, hinge from hips | Adductors, hamstrings, hip → **Side split** |
| 5 | Butterfly/groin | soles together, knees out | Short adductors, hip ext. rotators → **Side split support** |
| 6 | Downward dog (pedaling) | press heels; alternate bent/straight knee | Gastroc (straight) + soleus/Achilles (bent pedal); also hamstrings/shoulders/posterior chain → ankle (doubles hamstring) |

**Methods:** active end-range holds; PNF/contract-relax on split directions (straddle, hip-flexor lunge).

## PHASE 5 — Meditation (close of session)
_3–10 min._

| Element | How | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Posture | Seated tall or supine; eyes closed | Settles, decompresses spine |
| Breathing | Slow nasal, exhale longer than inhale (in 4 → out 6–8), 1–2 min min | Parasympathetic/vagal — long exhale is the active ingredient |
| Focus | Breath/body-scan anchor; return without judgement | Attentional control (sparring focus) |
| Sound (Klangschale) | Strike gently at start of each exhale; breathe out for the full fade. Strike to open & close | Auto-paces long exhale + attention anchor + ritual bracket |

**Klangschale note:** gentle strike so decay ≈ 6–8s exhale. Value = pacing/anchor/ritual, **NOT** "healing frequency" (no evidence).

**Why a phase:** training drives sympathetic; slow-exhale flips to parasympathetic recovery → nudges HRV, banks recovery Nightly Recharge measures.

## LITE Version (day-to-day, ~3 min → then full Phase 4)

1. Ankle + hip circles (2–3 ea.)
2. Spinal flow 1 pass + reverse
3. Leg swings 1 set ea.
4. Single-leg balance 30s/side

→ then **Phase 4 static block**. Phase 5 breathing optional but recommended.

---

## Safety

- **Scorpion over-the-top:** lumbar extension + rotation = highest low-back load. Hip-led, controlled, build gradually.
- **Neck rotations + refocus:** gentle; mild dizziness = vestibular stimulus, build slowly.
- **Knee circles:** hinge — small amplitude only.
- **Static before explosive work blunts power** — keep Phase 4 post-session.

---

## Coaching adaptations

- Cow stays in the flow; if broken into separate static pieces it's droppable for adults (cobra+puppy cover extension), keep for kids.
- Hip CARs: group default quadruped (easier to coach/correct); standing once pattern owned.
- Group reps: circles 3–5 (buy volume); 2–3 deliberate solo.
- Walking drills: travel for groups, march in place if tight on space.
