## Notes

### Intensity & Progression
- **W3 fast patterns:** Use crossover footwork and multi-direction exits for adults — compound the proprioceptive demand
- **W1 opposite-color variant:** Higher cognitive load; adults can handle 3+ colors + rule reversals without losing form
- **M2 Vestibular Dollyo:** This is youth/adults only. Add 2-rotation option and eyes-closed segment for intermediate athletes.
- **M3 eyes-closed:** Introduce gradually (session 2–3 once baseline established). This is the main progression lever.

### Group Dynamics (Youth/Adults)
- **W1 pairs with scorekeeping:** Track reaction errors/10 cues per person — appeals to competitive adults
- **M2 vestibular:** Partner watches gaze/landing quality — peer accountability is effective for adults
- **M4 Perturbation:** Group format works well — athletes observing count errors together, adds social pressure
- **M6 Gangpfad (if added):** Frame as elite protocol, not "balance game." Adults respond to the challenge framing.

### Silent Masking
Adults often mask balance difficulty. Provide partner accountability systems (gaze watches, landing checks) instead of self-reporting.

### Cool-Down Intensity
Extended C2 (12 min) for adults — build parasympathetic load with 10–12 breaths. Frame as preparation for competitive calm, not rest.

### Adaptations for Sparring-Heavy Groups
If your group does heavy sparring work, add 5 min of M1 Zahlen-Kombi with 3+ number sequences to prime working memory before sparring rounds. This builds automaticity in combination execution.
