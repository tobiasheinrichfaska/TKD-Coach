# Assessment System

Low-effort tracking that feels like training · retest rhythm · what progress looks like

---

## In-Game Individual Metrics

| Game | Metric | What to Track |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Balance Hold | Seconds, dominant + non-dominant | **Primary metric** — timed hold in kick-chamber position |
| W1 Farben-Chagi | Errors per 10 cues | Wrong kick or late response |
| M1 Zahlen-Kombi | Correct combos / attempts | Clean sequence execution on pads |
| M2 Vestibular Dollyo | Stable / Stumble / Fall per 5 reps | Landing quality post-rotation |
| M3 Einbein-Poomsae | Hold time + arm-technique errors | Seconds before foot touches + arm mistakes |
| M4 Poomsae unter Störung | Sequence errors vs. baseline | Errors when distractor present vs. clean baseline run |

---

## Group / Team Formats

- **Relay Farben-Chagi:** Team score = correct kicks per minute as relay chain (who completes most in 3 min?)
- **Team Balance Total:** Sum of all C1 hold times = session team score. Post it. Compare weekly.
- **Kombi Tournament:** Pairs compete — who completes a clean 4-combo sequence first, bracket format
- **Perturbation Grade:** Group counts each other's sequence errors. Lowest error count wins round.

---

## Retest Rhythm

| Interval | What to Retest | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Every 4–6 weeks | C1 Balance Hold (same position, same session slot) | Primary progress metric — 5–10s improvement over 6 weeks is significant |
| Every 6 weeks | W1 Farben-Chagi (errors/10 cues, 2 rounds) | Reaction speed improvement is observable early |
| Monthly | M4 Perturbation Poomsae (errors vs. clean baseline) | Consolidation of sequence under distraction builds monthly |

**Consistency is key:** Retest C1 under identical conditions (same time in session, same position, same footwear). Changes in context mask true progress.

## What "Better" Looks Like in TKD Terms

Observable signs of neuro-progress (not just numbers):

- **Faster first-movement after cue** in Farben-Chagi — visibly shorter lag before kick launches
- **No stumble-step** out of Vestibular Dollyo — landing absorbed cleanly into stance, hip stable
- **Poomsae sequence without freeze-pause** on next move — especially holding up under Perturbation pressure
- **Steadier single-leg chamber** — kick held longer before execution, no hip wobble on chamber
- **Eyes stay on opponent during lateral footwork** — observable in sparring rounds, not just drills
- **Kids specifically:** Less giggling/stumbling by rounds 4–6 of vestibular drills; more deliberate self-correction replacing pure reaction
- **Combination fluency:** Zahlen-Kombi sequences run without visible "internal search" between techniques — each move flows naturally
- **Balance hold time improvement:** +5–10s over 6 weeks is significant; +15s+ indicates strong vestibular/proprioceptive adaptation
- **Reduced distraction effect:** M4 errors under distraction approach M4 errors at baseline — sequence is more consolidated
- **Pre-fight calm:** Post-C2 gaze steadiness carries into early sparring rounds

---

## Notes on Retest Scheduling

**First session logged:** Pick a baseline date (e.g., 2026-05-28)

**Retest window:** 4–6 weeks later (e.g., 2026-06-25 to 2026-07-09)

Consistency of retest conditions is more important than exact weekly tracking. A single clean C1 hold every 6 weeks reveals progression more reliably than noisy daily measurements.
