# Flexibility Measures (monthly)

From the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md), Phase 4. Measure **monthly**, in the morning, after a light warm-up, under consistent conditions each time.

| Measure | Method | Unit | Übung |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standing forward fold | feet together, knees locked, reach down | cm to floor (neg = past floor) | [D-standing-forward-fold](../uebungen/dehnung/D-standing-forward-fold.md) |
| Sit-and-reach | seated, legs straight, reach past heels | cm past heel line | [D-seated-forward-fold](../uebungen/dehnung/D-seated-forward-fold.md) |
| Front split (L/R) | each leg forward, groin-to-floor gap | cm gap | [D-hip-flexor-lunge](../uebungen/dehnung/D-hip-flexor-lunge.md) |
| Side split | feet wide, groin-to-floor gap | cm gap | [D-straddle-pancake](../uebungen/dehnung/D-straddle-pancake.md) |

**Provisional baseline (2026-05-25):** forward fold 13 cm above floor · sit-and-reach +2 cm past heels · side split passive 31 cm gap. _Redo officially with tape measure + consistent morning routine._
