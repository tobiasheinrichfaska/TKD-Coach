## Notes

### Game Substitution
- **M2 Vestibular Dollyo is Youth/Adults only.** Substitute **M3 Einbein-Poomsae** for kids (already in this template).
- Kids respond better to color-naming and physical humor in W1 and W2.
- Expect more laughter/stumbling in M6 (Vestibular Gangpfad) if you add it — that's normal and adaptive.

### Group Dynamics (Kids)
- **Pairs for W1:** one calls, one kicks — keeps energy high
- **W3 ladder:** can run 2–3 kids through simultaneously with relay handoff
- **M1 Zahlen-Kombi:** pairs (kicker/pad-holder) — competitive group races work well
- **C1 Balance Hold:** announce times aloud at intervals to keep energy up, frame as "beat your last time"

### Form Policing
Kids will speed up for the neuro-challenge. Remind early and often: **form first, neuro-stimulus second.** A sloppy Dollyo fast is not progress.

### Energy Management
- Place easier/re-engagement games (M5 Reaktions-Zahl-Kreis, W2 mirror) mid-session if group fatigue shows
- Save C1 balance hold as the main measurement moment — it's the primary weekly metric
