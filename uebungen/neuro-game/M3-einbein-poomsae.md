# M3 · Einbein-Poomsae

*Single-leg Poomsae — proprioceptive overload*

**Main** · **12 min** · **All ages** · **Proprioceptive + Vestibular**

---

## Neuro-Target

Single-leg postural control under dual cognitive load: executing a Poomsae arm sequence while the proprioceptive system manages a dynamic single-leg stance. The two tasks compete for cerebellar resources — exactly the condition of executing technique under pressure.

## Taekwondo Technique

Poomsae arm segments (blocks and strikes) from the athlete's current Poomsae — full correct form, not simplified. Standing on one leg changes the proprioceptive demand, not the arm technique.

✓ **Poomsae arm technique — form preserved and actively trained**

## TKD Link

Poomsae precision under pressure · kick chamber balance · single-leg stability for all kicks

## Setup

Yoga mats or similar (for safety). No other equipment. Can run individually or in lines.

## How to Play

1. Athletes stand on one leg (non-dominant first), free leg in chamber position
2. Execute Poomsae arm sequence (3–5 moves) in correct timing and form
3. Coach counts errors in arm technique AND balance breaks
4. Switch legs after each sequence
5. Progress: eyes closed when stable with eyes open (add slowly)

## Variations

| ↓ Kids/Beginners | ↑ Advanced |
|---|---|
| Wall touch allowed for first 2 attempts · shorter 2-move sequence · dominant leg only first | Eyes closed · partner creates peripheral visual distraction · unstable surface (foam pad) |

## Log Metric

Hold time (seconds) before foot touches down — this is the primary retest metric. Note separately: arm technique errors vs. balance breaks.

## Why It Works Neurologically

The cerebellum handles both proprioceptive balance regulation and motor sequencing. Loading both simultaneously forces more efficient neural processing — the system learns to run the arm program with less active attention, freeing cognitive bandwidth. Eyes-closed removes visual compensation, pushing the vestibular and proprioceptive systems to do more work independently.
