# W2 · Spiegel-Stand

*Mirror stance — visual-proprioceptive mirroring*

**Warm-up** · **5 min** · **All ages** · **Visual + Proprioceptive**

---

## Neuro-Target

Visual + proprioceptive integration. Mirroring suppresses prediction and forces continuous sensory processing — the anti-habit stimulus. Also trains anticipation reading without acting on it prematurely.

## Taekwondo Technique

Ap Seogi · Juchum Seogi · step-switches · Makki blocks (Arae, Momtong, Olgul). Form policed: call out any stance collapse for speed.

✓ **Stances and blocks — form preserved**

## TKD Link

Sparring distance management · opponent-reading · Poomsae stance precision under movement

## How to Play

1. Pairs face each other, 1.5m apart, both in Ap Seogi
2. Leader changes stances and adds blocks at will — no announcement
3. Partner mirrors instantly (mirror image, not copy)
4. Coach calls "switch" every 30–45s — roles swap
5. Remind athletes: form first, speed second

## Variations

| ↓ Easier | ↑ Harder |
|---|---|
| Slow tempo · only 2 stance options · same height throughout | Add direction steps · "freeze" cue (hold 3s, coach checks form) · add kicks |

## Why It Works Neurologically

Continuous visual input drives proprioceptive adjustment in real-time — the mirror neuron system is activated alongside genuine sensorimotor recalibration. The freeze cue forces the cerebellum to lock in positional accuracy, which transfers directly to Poomsae hold positions.
