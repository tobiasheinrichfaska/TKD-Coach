# M5 · Reaktions-Zahl-Kreis

*Auditory cue → block selection — group re-engagement tool*

**Main** · **8 min** · **All ages** · **Auditory Reaction + Attention**

---

## Neuro-Target

Auditory cue processing → block selection → correct motor execution. Trains the auditory-motor pathway independently of visual input. Also a powerful group attention-reset tool.

## Taekwondo Technique

Makki blocks: 1 = Arae Makki · 2 = Momtong Makki · 3 = Olgul Makki. Full hip rotation, correct arm path, no shortcutting for speed.

✓ **Makki blocks — form preserved and emphasized**

## TKD Link

Block selection in sparring · combination blocking · reaction to attack patterns

## How to Play

1. Athletes form a circle, fighting stance, weight centered
2. Coach calls a number (1, 2, or 3) — all execute the matching block simultaneously
3. Wrong block or late = small penalty (5 pushups or 1 round sit-out)
4. Increase call speed progressively. Mix in false starts (long pause → sudden call).

## Variations

| ↓ Easier | ↑ Harder |
|---|---|
| 2 numbers only · slow cadence · visual card alongside number | 4 options · call in Korean TKD terms · elimination round |

## Why It Works Neurologically

Isolating auditory input as the sole trigger trains an independent sensorimotor pathway. Many TKD athletes are visually dominant — building an equally fast auditory-motor reflex broadens the neural options available in sparring. The social/penalty element adds mild stress, which is a valid neuro-stressor that mimics competition arousal states.
