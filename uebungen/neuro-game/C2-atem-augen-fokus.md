# C2 · Atem-Augen-Fokus

*Breathing + gaze — parasympathetic cool-down*

**Cool-Down** · **6–12 min** · **All ages** · **Parasympathetic + Gaze**

---

## Neuro-Target

Parasympathetic nervous system activation (via extended exhale) + gaze stabilization. Brings HR and arousal back to baseline while training focused fixation — the same visual discipline needed for pre-fight calm.

## Taekwondo Technique

Low Juchum Seogi (horse stance) held throughout. Correct depth, correct spine, arms in resting guard position. Stance is trained — this is not passive rest.

✓ **Juchum Seogi — stance held and monitored**

> ⚠ **Non-TKD protocol:** The breathing protocol (4-in / 6-out extended exhale) is non-TKD — no TKD technique achieves this specific parasympathetic trigger. Justified for cool-down neuro-function.

## How to Run

1. Take Juchum Seogi — coach checks depth and alignment
2. Fix gaze on one point on the wall at eye level — maintain throughout
3. Breathe: 4 counts in through nose, 6 counts out through mouth
4. Kids: 6–8 breaths. Adults: 10–12 breaths.
5. Silent. Coach watches stance — correct any collapse quietly.

## Why It Works Neurologically

Extended exhale activates the vagus nerve and shifts the autonomic nervous system toward parasympathetic dominance — reducing cortisol, lowering HR, and beginning the recovery window. Simultaneous gaze fixation consolidates the VOR improvements made during the session. Performing this in a held TKD stance means the session ends with correct proprioceptive feedback, not collapse-posture.
