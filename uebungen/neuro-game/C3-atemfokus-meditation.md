# C3 · Atem-Fokus / Meditation

*Phase 5 of the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md).*

**How:** Seated tall or supine, eyes closed. Slow nasal breathing, exhale longer than inhale (in 4 → out 6–8), ~5–6 breaths/min, 1–2 min minimum. Optional Klangschale: gentle strike at the start of each exhale, breathe out for the full ~6–8 s fade.

**Dose:** 3–10 min, close of session.

**Why:** Long exhale is the active ingredient — flips sympathetic→parasympathetic recovery (vagal), supports HRV. Klangschale paces the exhale and anchors attention (ritual, not 'healing frequency').
