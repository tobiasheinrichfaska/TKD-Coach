# M2 · Vestibular Dollyo

*Post-rotation balance → full kick execution*

**Main** · **12 min** · **Youth + Adults** · **Vestibular Recalibration**

---

## Neuro-Target

Vestibular recalibration: the system must re-establish spatial orientation and stable chamber immediately after rotation — training the reflex that holds spinning-kick landings and lateral cut recoveries.

## Taekwondo Technique

Full Dollyo Chagi — chamber, extension, snap retraction, controlled landing. The kick is never modified. The pre-kick rotation is the vestibular stimulus.

✓ **Dollyo Chagi — full technique preserved**

> ⚠ **Non-TKD stimulus:** The rotation (1–2 spins) is deliberate non-TKD stimulus — justified because no TKD technique generates the same vestibular perturbation. Kick remains authentic. Flag this to athletes.

## TKD Link

Spinning kick stability · lateral direction change recovery · balance under spatial disorientation

## Setup

Kick shields at 2–3 stations. Pairs (shield holder + kicker). Open floor 3m per station.

## How to Play

1. Kicker stands facing shield, arms out, spotting a point on wall
2. Execute 1 full controlled rotation (spotting on wall)
3. On landing: immediate Dollyo Chagi to shield — no pause, no wobble allowed
4. Hold the landing 2s before lowering foot
5. Rotate after 5 reps. Coach watches for stable hips on kick, not just balance.

## Variations

| ↓ Easier | ↑ Harder |
|---|---|
| No rotation — close eyes 3s before kick · one-foot stand before kick | 2 rotations · eyes-closed segment · coach calls target height (low/mid/high) |

## Log Metric

Per set of 5: stable landing / stumble / foot down. Track as S/T/F ratio. "Better" = more S, fewer T and F over weeks.

## Why It Works Neurologically

Rotation creates endolymph displacement in the semicircular canals, generating a vestibular signal inconsistent with visual input. The nervous system must rapidly recalibrate to execute a controlled motor act. Repeated exposure trains the cerebellum to filter vestibular noise faster — the same mechanism that makes experienced spinning-kick athletes land cleanly.
