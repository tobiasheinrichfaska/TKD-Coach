# Standing Forward Fold

*From the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md). ⚠ Never stretch cold or before explosive work — post-session or when warm.*

**How:** Hinge and hang; let the spine round, knees soft→straight.

**Dose:** 30–60 s

**Covers:** Posterior chain: hamstrings, gastroc, erectors.

**Tracked measure:** Forward fold (cm to floor)
