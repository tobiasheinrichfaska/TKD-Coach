# Half-Kneeling Hip-Flexor Lunge

*From the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md). ⚠ Never stretch cold or before explosive work — post-session or when warm.*

**How:** Half-kneel, tuck pelvis, optional reach back. PNF contract-relax.

**Dose:** 30–60 s / side

**Covers:** Iliopsoas + rectus femoris; anterior trunk.

**Tracked measure:** Front split (cm gap, L/R)
