# Downward Dog (pedaling)

*From the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md). ⚠ Never stretch cold or before explosive work — post-session or when warm.*

**How:** Press heels; alternate bent/straight knee (pedal).

**Dose:** 30–60 s

**Covers:** Gastroc (straight) + soleus/Achilles (bent); hamstrings/shoulders.
