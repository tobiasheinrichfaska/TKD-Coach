# Knee-to-Chest + Heel-to-Glute

*From the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md).*

**How:** Alternate knee-to-chest hug and heel-to-glute. Tall posture.

**Dose:** ~10 m walking (or march in place)

**Covers:** Hip flexion (glutes/hamstring); quad/hip flexor + knee flexion.
