# Scorpion (dynamic)

*From the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md).*

**How:** Hip-led reach of the foot across; controlled. ⚠ highest low-back load — build gradually.

**Dose:** 5–6 / side

**Covers:** Hip + lumbar/thoracic spine (multi-plane); hip flexors, glutes, erectors, obliques.
