# Hip CARs (quadruped)

*From the [Mobility / Warm-Up / Stretching protocol](../../session-templates/ST-mobility-stretching-protocol.md).*

**How:** Largest pain-free hip circle; keep the spine still (irradiation).

**Dose:** 2–3 each side — slow, spine quiet

**Covers:** Hip + capsule; glutes, iliopsoas, adductors, deep external rotators.
